ISLE RECORDS — Music Isle: Store Side Entry Update

This package preserves the current Music Isle and its audio assets. The store garden walk now passes along the side wall of the Isle Store, rounds the front corner, and terminates at a short timber ramp that connects to the wooden showcase deck from the side. The store terrain pad has been extended slightly to support a level approach and ramp landing.

Upload index.html together with the complete assets/ folder. Three.js is loaded from public CDNs, so an internet connection is required. A live WebGL render test was not available in this environment.

Update: Added four instanced wooden stair flights at the Release Pier branch, Store garden branch, Harbour approach, and Music Isle approach. Stair treads and risers sample the existing road curves and the shared H(x,z) height field, and are batched with InstancedMesh for performance.
